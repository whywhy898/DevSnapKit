Frequently asked questions.

Is DevSnapKit free?

Yes. The studio, device frames, Duo layouts, 3D angles, text, logo uploads, QR codes and PNG, WebP and JPEG exports are free. DevSnapKit Pro is a one-time payment of $1 USD for lifetime access to premium templates, the 4K export preset, batch export and premium gradient backgrounds. There is no subscription.

Are my screenshots uploaded to a server?

No. Editing and image rendering happen locally in your browser, so your screenshots stay on your device while you style and export them.

Do I need an account or design skills?

No. Open the studio and start editing; there is nothing to sign up for. Built-in demo screenshots let you try every control before you bring your own image.

Which sizes can I export?

You can switch the canvas between formats such as Open Graph (1200×630), X/Twitter, LinkedIn, 16:9 and YouTube, square, and Instagram post and story. Exports are available as PNG, WebP or JPEG.

Does DevSnapKit capture screenshots for me?

Start with an image you have already captured. DevSnapKit styles and composes that image; it does not capture a website from its URL.
