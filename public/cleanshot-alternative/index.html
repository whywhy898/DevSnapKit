A CleanShot X alternative for styling screenshots

What CleanShot X is

CleanShot X is a native macOS app (macOS 13 or later; there is no Windows version). It handles screenshots, screen recording, annotation, a Background Tool and CleanShot Cloud sharing. Its pricing page lists a $35 one-time Basic license with a year of updates, a Pro plan at $10 per user per month billed annually, and availability through Setapp. If you want an all-in-one Mac capture tool, it is a strong choice.

Why some people pick DevSnapKit instead

  • Any platform. It is a web page, so Windows and Linux users can use it.
  • Focused on presentation. Device frames (iPhone 17 Pro, MacBook Pro, Studio Display), 3D angles, gradients and social-size presets.
  • Free, no watermark. Pro is a one-time $1 for 4K and batch export.
  • No upload. Images are rendered in your browser tab and are not uploaded.

DevSnapKit does not capture, record or annotate with arrows — keep your capture tool for that and use DevSnapKit for the final image.

Questions

Can I use both?

Yes. Capture with CleanShot X or your OS tool, then drop the image into DevSnapKit for frames and social sizes.

Does DevSnapKit store my screenshots?

No. Editing and export happen in your browser and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Is there a Windows version of DevSnapKit?

There is no app to install. The browser editor works the same on Windows, macOS and Linux.
