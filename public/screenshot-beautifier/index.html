A few things worth knowing.

What is a screenshot beautifier?

A screenshot beautifier adds presentation around an existing screenshot: a background, spacing, rounded corners, shadows, or a frame. DevSnapKit helps you arrange these elements in your browser.

Does DevSnapKit take screenshots for me?

Start with an image you have already captured. DevSnapKit styles and composes that image; it does not automatically capture a website from its URL.

Are my screenshots uploaded to a server?

Screenshot editing and image rendering happen locally in your browser. You do not need to upload your screenshot to a rendering server to style it.

Do I need Figma or a design background?

No. Open the studio, choose an image, and use the canvas and frame controls. Built-in demo screenshots let you try the workflow before bringing your own image.

Can I use it for social posts and launch graphics?

Yes. Adjust the canvas aspect ratio and compose a visual for your post, tutorial, or launch. Check the destination platform's current image specifications before publishing.

Is every feature free?

DevSnapKit offers an editor you can try and additional Pro options. Check the current Pro panel in the studio for available features and purchase terms before buying.
