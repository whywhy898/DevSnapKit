MacBook mockup generator for websites and apps

Make it in four steps

  1. Capture the page at a laptop-like size; 1280×800 or 1440×900 fills the screen without letterboxing.
  2. Open the studio, drop in the image and choose MacBook Pro in Frame → Device styles. For a desktop monitor look, choose Studio Display instead.
  3. Pick a background under Canvas: a gradient, a solid brand color, a subtle dot or grid pattern, or your own photo.
  4. Choose 16:9 for slides and video thumbnails, Twitter/OG for link previews, or a custom size, then download.

Getting a natural result

Keep the screenshot close to 16:10 so it fills the display. Use a front-flat angle for documentation and a 3D angle such as Floating Tilt or 3D Horizon for marketing. Darker gradients make a silver laptop pop; light backgrounds suit a dark-mode UI. If you also have a phone screenshot, Duo mode can place both side by side.

Prefer a lighter frame? A browser window mockup keeps attention on the page itself.

Common uses

  • Landing page hero — show the product in context above the fold.
  • Portfolio and case studies — present client sites consistently.
  • Slides and pitch decks — a 16:9 export drops straight into a slide.
  • YouTube thumbnails — the YouTube preset is 1280×720.

Everything is rendered locally in your browser, so unreleased designs and client work never leave your computer.

Questions

Which MacBook model is the frame based on?

The frame is labelled MacBook Pro in the editor. It is a stylised render, not an official Apple asset.

What resolution can I export?

Free exports go up to 2x the canvas size, for example 3840×2160 from the 16:9 preset. The $9.9 Pro pass adds a 4K Ultra export option.

Can I change the laptop angle?

Yes. Six 3D presets are available, from Front Flat to Dynamic Skew.
