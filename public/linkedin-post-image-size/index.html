LINKEDIN IMAGE SIZE

LinkedIn post image size for screenshots

LinkedIn accepts a wide range of shapes. These are the numbers LinkedIn publishes, and the presets that match them in DevSnapKit.

What LinkedIn publishes

ItemSpec
Photo postsAt least 552×276; 1080 px wide recommended; aspect ratio between 3:1 and 4:5; up to 5MB; up to 20 images
Link preview imageAt least 1200×627 (1.91:1), up to 5MB
Single image ads1200×628, 1200×1200 or 720×900

Sources: LinkedIn Help — sharing photos, link previews, image ad specs. Last checked November 2026.

Matching presets

  • LinkedIn Post (1200×627) — landscape, matches the link-preview ratio.
  • 1:1 Square (1080×1080) — a common choice that holds up on mobile.
  • Instagram Post (1080×1350) — 4:5, the tallest ratio LinkedIn allows, so it takes the most room in the feed.

LinkedIn does not publish one ideal pixel size for organic posts, so pick by layout: wide desktop UI suits 1200×627; a phone screen suits 4:5. Open the studio, choose the preset under Export → Canvas Aspect Ratio, and keep text large enough to read on a phone. For X, see the X post image size guide.

Readability on LinkedIn

Most LinkedIn browsing happens on mobile, so a dense desktop screenshot shrinks quickly. Crop to the part of the interface you are talking about, add a short headline with the text tool, and keep the background quiet. Images with a clear single focus tend to be easier to understand at a glance than full-page captures.

Questions

What is the best LinkedIn image size?

For a tall, attention-grabbing post use 1080×1350 (4:5). For a wide screenshot use 1200×627. Both are within LinkedIn’s published range.

Is the LinkedIn preset free?

Yes. All size presets and custom sizes are free.

Can I export several sizes at once?

Batch multi-size export is part of the $1 Pro pass.
