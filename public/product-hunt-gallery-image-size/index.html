Product Hunt gallery image size, and how to hit it

Product Hunt’s launch guide recommends 1270×760 pixels for gallery images. Here are the official numbers and a quick way to export at exactly that size.

The official specs

AssetRecommended
Gallery images1270×760 px; at least 2 needed for the gallery to appear
ThumbnailSquare, 240×240 px; GIFs allowed
File sizeUnder 3MB per image

Source: Product Hunt — Preparing for launch. Last checked November 2026.

Exporting at 1270×760

  1. Open the studio and import your screenshot.
  2. Go to Export → Canvas Aspect Ratio, type 1270 for width and 760 for height, and press Set.
  3. Style it: a frame, a background and a short headline with the text tool.
  4. Download at 1x for exactly 1270×760. WebP or JPEG usually keeps the file well under 3MB.

What to put in the gallery

The first gallery image works like a thumbnail for your whole launch, so lead with the clearest view of the product and a short benefit headline. Follow with one feature per image, a before-and-after if your product changes a workflow, and a final image that says how to start. Keep every image at the same size and background so the gallery feels like one story. Text should be large enough to read when the image is scaled down in the feed.

Questions

Is 1270×760 required?

It is Product Hunt’s recommendation. Images at the same 127:76 ratio scale cleanly; other ratios may be cropped or letterboxed.

Can I export the thumbnail too?

Yes. Set a custom 240×240 canvas. Keep it simple — it is shown very small.

Should I export at 2x?

Only if the file stays under 3MB. 2x gives 2540×1520, which looks sharper on high-density screens.
