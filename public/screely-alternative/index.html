What happened to Screely?

When we checked in November 2026, screely.com showed a hosting provider’s default page rather than the editor. A separate site, screely.app, offers a free screenshot mockup tool that says it adds no watermark and processes images locally. We could not confirm whether it is connected to the original Screely, so we do not compare against it here.

Doing the same job in DevSnapKit

  1. Open the studio and drop in your screenshot.
  2. In Frame → Device styles, pick Safari Browser, macOS Light, macOS Dark or Glass Window, and edit the window title.
  3. Under Canvas, choose a gradient, solid color, pattern or your own background image.
  4. Pick a size — Auto, 16:9, 1:1, Twitter/OG, LinkedIn or a custom width and height — and download.

Free exports have no watermark, and the work happens in your browser without uploading the image.

Who this is for

If you mostly need a quick, tidy browser screenshot for a README, a blog post or a client update, DevSnapKit keeps that flow short: no account, no install, no upload. If you need more, the same editor adds device frames, 3D angles, text, logo and social-size presets when you want them.

Questions

Is DevSnapKit made by the Screely team?

No. DevSnapKit is an independent tool. We mention Screely only because many people searched for it.

Can I export a transparent background?

Yes. Choose the Alpha background and export PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Does it cost anything?

The core editor is free with no watermark. Pro is an optional $9.9 one-time payment.
