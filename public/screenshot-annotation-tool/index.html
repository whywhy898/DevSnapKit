From screenshot to clear explanation

  1. Open the studio and choose or drop an image. No account is required.
  2. Open Elements and choose the annotation tools. Place an arrow, box or highlight over the part you want someone to notice.
  3. Add a short text label for the action or detail. Move and resize the elements so they remain easy to read.
  4. Export a PNG, WebP or JPEG. Free exports have no watermark.

One detail at a time

Use an arrow to point to a button, a box to frame a group of controls, or a highlight to draw attention to a number. Keep labels brief and leave enough contrast against the screenshot. Annotation works well for support replies, bug reports, onboarding steps and release notes.

For a redesign, arrange two images with the before-and-after screenshot maker, then label the difference. Add a frame and background with the screenshot beautifier when your explanation needs a finished presentation.

Your images stay with you

DevSnapKit edits and renders locally. Your screenshots are never uploaded to a rendering server. Annotation and standard exports are free, with no sign-up and no watermark. Pro lifetime access is an optional one-time $9.9 purchase for additional export and template options.

DevSnapKit styles existing screenshots. Capture the screen with your system tool first, then bring the image into the editor.

Questions

Can I add arrows, boxes and highlights?

Yes. Use the annotation tools for arrows, boxes and highlights, and the text tool for labels. You can move and resize them on the canvas.

Is screenshot annotation free?

Yes. Annotate and export without a watermark or an account. Optional Pro lifetime access costs $9.9 for additional export and template options.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. Editing and export happen locally in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.

Can I use it on Windows or a phone?

Yes. Open DevSnapKit in a modern browser on Windows, macOS, Linux or mobile. No app installation is required.
