How it works

  1. Import a tall screenshot. When an image is more than about 15% taller than it is wide, the studio switches to the iPhone 17 Pro frame for you.
  2. Adjust the frame. Under Frame → Device styles you can also choose the stylised Clay Phone frame instead, and use the 3D angle presets (Isometric Left, Floating Tilt and others) for depth.
  3. Set the canvas. In Export → Canvas Aspect Ratio, 1:1 suits feeds, Instagram Post (1080×1350) and Story (1080×1920) suit vertical placements, or type a custom size.
  4. Export. PNG, WebP or JPEG at 1x or 2x for free.

Tips for a clean phone mockup

Capture at your device’s native resolution so text stays sharp inside the frame — a modern iPhone screenshot is roughly 1170–1320 pixels wide. Hide the status bar clutter before capturing if you can. To show two screens together, switch to Duo mode and import a second screenshot; Overlap Stack and Side by Side layouts work well for onboarding flows.

Building store listings? See the App Store screenshot guide for Apple’s exact sizes.

Where phone mockups help

A framed phone screenshot reads instantly as “this is an app”, which matters in places where people scroll fast: launch posts, the hero of a landing page, an investor deck or a changelog entry. Framing also hides inconsistent screenshot edges and makes different screens look like one set. Keep the background calm and on-brand, and let one screen carry one message — a crowded collage is harder to read at feed size than a single, clear device.

Questions

Which iPhone frames are available?

An iPhone 17 Pro frame and a stylised Clay Phone frame. There are no Android device frames at the moment.

Is the iPhone mockup free?

Yes. Device frames and exports up to 2x are free with no watermark. 4K export is part of the $1 Pro pass.

Can I use the mockup commercially?

Yes. You own the images you create, including for app stores, client work and ads.
