What Shots.so does well

Shots.so is a browser-based mockup editor. Its site highlights free mockups, animated mockups with video zoom, iPhone mockups including iPhone 17, templates, “magic” backgrounds, 3D shapes, filters, social media export presets and shareable links. If you need animated or video output, it offers things DevSnapKit does not.

Where DevSnapKit fits

DevSnapKit focuses on still images. You drop in a screenshot, pick a device style (iPhone 17 Pro, MacBook Pro, Studio Display, Safari browser or macOS window), choose a background and export PNG, WebP or JPEG. Free exports have no watermark and go up to 2x resolution. A one-time $1 Pro pass adds 4K export, batch multi-size export, premium gradients and premium templates — there is no subscription.

Everything is drawn on a canvas in your browser tab. Our privacy policy describes this as a zero-upload design: your screenshots are not sent to our servers.

Questions

Is DevSnapKit free like Shots.so?

Yes. The editor, all device frames, 12 gradients and exports up to 2x are free with no watermark. Pro is optional and costs $1 once.

Can DevSnapKit make animated mockups?

No. DevSnapKit exports still PNG, WebP and JPEG images. For animated or video mockups, Shots.so is the better fit.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the studio and start editing. The Pro pass is unlocked with a license key rather than an account.
