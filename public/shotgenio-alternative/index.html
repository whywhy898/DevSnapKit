A ShotGenio alternative for clean, still mockups

What ShotGenio offers

ShotGenio is a web-based screenshot mockup maker with a Chrome extension. Its site highlights animated stickers, animated sunlight effects, a motion studio for GIF and MP4 backgrounds, iPhone 17 Pro and MacBook Pro 14″ looks, 9:16 story formats and AI-generated post captions. Its pricing page lists a free plan that exports with a watermark, a one-time Lifetime Editor (shown at $9.99 during a launch sale) and optional AI caption credits.

What you get with DevSnapKit

DevSnapKit sticks to still images. The free editor includes iPhone 17 Pro, MacBook Pro, Studio Display, Safari and macOS frames, six 3D angle presets, gradient/solid/pattern/image backgrounds, text, buttons, logo and QR elements, and presets for OG, Instagram, Story, LinkedIn and YouTube sizes. Exports up to 2x carry no watermark.

Pro is $9.9 once for 4K, batch export, premium gradients and premium templates. There is no AI writing and no animation; images are processed in your browser and never uploaded.

Choosing between them

If motion and AI captions are central to how you post, ShotGenio is built around them. If you mostly need sharp still images for docs, store listings and launch posts — and prefer that screenshots never leave your machine — DevSnapKit is a simple fit.

Questions

Can DevSnapKit make animated mockups?

No. It exports still PNG, WebP and JPEG files. For GIF or MP4 motion, ShotGenio is the better fit.

Does DevSnapKit write captions with AI?

No. You type your own headline with the text tool.

Are my screenshots uploaded?

No. DevSnapKit renders everything in your browser tab.
