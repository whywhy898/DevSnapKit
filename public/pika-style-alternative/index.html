A Pika.style alternative without a watermark or subscription

Pika.style (the screenshot beautifier at pika.style, not the Pika video app) is a well-rounded browser tool. DevSnapKit is a lighter option if you mainly want clean, free exports.

What Pika.style offers

Pika.style runs in the browser and also ships a Chrome extension, a macOS app, a VS Code extension and a Raycast extension. It can capture a website from a URL and has templates for tweets, code, App Store screenshots and device mockups. You can use it without an account; free exports include a “designed in Pika.style” watermark, which Pro removes. Its pricing page lists Pro at $15 a month or $150 a year, and a $299 lifetime plan.

How DevSnapKit differs

DevSnapKit starts from an image you already have — it does not capture URLs. In exchange, the free tier is complete enough for most launches: all device frames, 12 gradients plus custom two-color gradients, solid, pattern and image backgrounds, text, logo and QR elements, and PNG/WebP/JPEG export up to 2x with no watermark.

Pro is a single $9.9 payment for 4K export, batch multi-size export, premium gradients and templates. Rendering stays in your tab, so screenshots are never uploaded.

Questions

Is this related to Pika, the AI video app?

No. This page compares DevSnapKit with Pika.style, the screenshot and mockup tool at pika.style.

Can I capture a website by URL in DevSnapKit?

No. Take a screenshot first, then drop it into the studio. Pika.style supports URL capture if you need it.

What does the $9.9 Pro pass include?

Lifetime access to 4K export, batch multi-size export, premium gradients and premium templates. No recurring charges.
